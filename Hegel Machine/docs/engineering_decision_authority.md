# Engineering decision authority

Effective update: 2026-09-07.

This file records how the project owner's objectives, research decisions and
specialized formal contracts govern Hegel Machine work.

The owner explicitly selected an overall change of direction toward cognitive
research, beginning with the Markdown architecture and runnable code skeleton.
This direction is authorized work. Its scope is defined by the current
[README](../README.md), [architecture](architecture.md) and
[cognitive research roadmap](cognitive_research_roadmap.md).

## Precedence and applicability

When inputs conflict, apply this order within the operation being performed:

1. the project owner's current explicit objective, constraints and authorization;
2. the current project architecture and the specific contract applicable to the
   requested research or formal operation;
3. observed executable behavior, relevant tests and produced research evidence;
4. documented engineering judgment for implementation details;
5. historical discussions, webpage GPT proposals, estimates and review comments.

Historical formal identities, signed evidence, frozen datasets and published
results retain their identity. An explicit change of research direction changes
current priorities and architecture; it does not rewrite those historical facts.
A formal contract governs its named operation and claim, rather than every
experiment in the project.

## Current cognitive research mainline

- Carry the owner's authorized direction through to concrete documents and
  running implementation. A historical advisory-only label does not turn the
  owner's current explicit choice back into an unapproved suggestion.
- Resolve routine design choices using the actual repository, the research
  objective and engineering judgment. Record material choices in the current
  architecture or roadmap where they affect future work.
- Prioritize real candidate construction, informative experiments, useful
  revision and knowledge reuse. Choose between implementations by correctness,
  explanatory clarity, execution cost and research value for the supported task.
- Permit competing diagnoses, joint and multistep revisions, recursive
  subproblems and experimental knowledge updates under a shared budget.
  A complete old-language closure or formal certificate is not a prerequisite
  for these research operations.
- Use the checks that can detect a relevant failure and change the next action.
  Ordinary reversible edits do not require new amendment documents, security
  scaffolding or tests that merely mirror the implementation.
- Keep the external task objective and resource accounting explicit. Candidate
  usefulness follows actual outcomes, including failures and costs.
- Distinguish a runnable mechanism from an observed research effect. State
  results plainly and collect remaining capability limitations in the roadmap's
  Known Issues.

## Specialized formal work

Exact wire, cross-language replay, custody, signatures, sealed data and issuance
rules continue to apply when performing their corresponding formal operations.
A research controller's local knowledge state is separate from formal
certificate issuance or an externally governed ACTIVE state.

For changes to a frozen formal contract, use its required amendment and
verification process. The Phase-3A M2.5 precedent remains
[Implementation Closure Addendum v1](Hegel_Machine_Phase3A_M25_Implementation_Closure_Addendum_v1.md).
Its identity and trust requirements concern that formal track; they are not the
default tie-breaker for cognitive research design.

External publishing, custody, credential use and other separately controlled
actions follow the owner's authorization and the applicable operation's rules.
The current direction change authorizes local architecture and implementation
work; it does not by itself issue an external certificate or publish a result.

## Recording a material decision

Record the research problem, the selected behavior, why it serves that problem,
and the evidence needed to assess it. Include affected interfaces or contracts
when they matter. Verification should address the actual failure under study.

Use the current document when it is the natural home for that decision. Create
a separate versioned formal amendment only when the specific contract requires
one. Commits and publication follow the owner's requested workflow.
