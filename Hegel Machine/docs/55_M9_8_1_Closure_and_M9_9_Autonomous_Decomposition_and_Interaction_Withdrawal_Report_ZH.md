### 8.2 零种子分解下的精确组合摊销定律（`|U| · |D|` vs `|U| + |D| - 1 + m_c`）与短周期盈亏平衡解析

$$N_{\text{mono\_in\_stream}} = |U| \cdot |D| - 1, \qquad N_{\text{hegel\_in\_stream}} = (|U| - 1) + (|D| - 1) + m_c, \qquad N_{\text{sep\_unseen}} = (|U| - 1)(|D| - 1) - m_c.$$
