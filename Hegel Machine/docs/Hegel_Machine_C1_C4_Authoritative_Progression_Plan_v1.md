This document is a prospective execution and evidence plan. It is not a
readiness receipt, an authorization, an execution record, an actual-corpus
result, or scientific evidence that any C1-C4 claim has passed.

```text
C1 authoritative actual-960 PASS
  -> C2 untouched-relation invention PASS
  -> C3 multi-generation retention/reduction/rollback PASS

```

No downstream stage may start its claim-bearing attempt before every upstream
stage has a verified authoritative PASS. Local schema, verifier, fixture, and
negative-test construction may proceed in advance, but those artifacts remain
candidate mechanics and cannot satisfy an execution gate.

## 3. Global invariants

Every claim-bearing stage must enforce all of the following:

1. Freeze the specification, schemas, code identities, statistical gates,
   retry policy, and claim vocabulary before a fresh attempt is allocated.
2. Allocate campaign and attempt identifiers only through the admitted launch
   authority for that stage.
3. Bind input roots, implementation roots, dependency roots, key registry,
   environment identity, and attempt identity before prediction or synthesis.
4. Record append-only commitment, execution, reveal, scoring, and terminal
   events under distinct roles and signature purposes.
5. Preserve every FAIL, REJECTED, INVALIDATED, timeout, crash, and missing
   output in the attempt denominator. A failed holdout is never silently
   deleted, relabelled, or reused as a fresh holdout.
6. Permit a retry only under the preregistered infrastructure-failure rule,
   with a new attempt identity and immutable linkage to the failed attempt.
7. Verify content addresses and canonical raw bytes before interpreting
   decoded values. Never accept caller-supplied decoded authority booleans.
8. Keep local signatures, fixtures, Docker success, fsync mechanics, and
   producer-free replay separate from external identity, currentness,
   durability, custody, and scientific validity.
9. Stop immediately on the earliest failed prerequisite. Downstream evidence
   produced after an invalid predecessor is non-admissible.
10. Treat stage completion as false until an independent verifier accepts the
    complete raw evidence graph and the permanent nonclaims remain exact.

Every claim-bearing attempt terminates in exactly one disposition family:

- `PASS`: a complete valid run whose scientific and operational gates pass;
- `COMPLETE_FAIL`: a complete valid run whose frozen scientific or performance
  gate fails, with the complete unfavorable result retained;
- `REJECTED` or `INVALIDATED`: authority, custody, chronology, leakage,
  canonicality, or integrity failure; no partial score is admissible;
- `NOT_RUN` or `BLOCKED`: an unmet predecessor prevented the attempt from
  starting. Missing execution is neither a pass nor a scientific failure.

### 4.5 C1 stop conditions

Before campaign and attempt IDs exist, any false R01-R13 predecessor yields
`NOT_RUN` or `BLOCKED`; it does not create or terminate an attempt. After a
valid claim-bearing attempt exists, a complete run that misses a frozen
scientific, statistical, preservation, or performance threshold terminates as
`COMPLETE_FAIL` and retains the unfavorable result. Signature, authority,
custody, chronology, leakage, canonicality, stale/reused-ID, denominator,
unauthorized-retry, corpus-integrity, or scorer-integrity failures terminate as
`REJECTED` or `INVALIDATED`. C2 remains locked unless the terminal C1
disposition is authoritative `PASS`.

Each permitted statistical-validation version has at most attempt `0` and,
conditionally, attempt `1`. Attempt `1` is allowed only when attempt `0`
records action `REEXECUTE`, `any_raw_prediction_byte_produced=false`, exactly
zero raw-output bytes, and exactly one of these reasons:

- `CONTAINER_START_FAILURE`;
- `HOST_OOM_BEFORE_FIRST_PREDICTION_BYTE`;
- `HOST_TERMINATION_BEFORE_FIRST_PREDICTION_BYTE`;
- `INPUT_TRANSFER_CHECKSUM_MISMATCH`.

Attempt `1` must terminate as `COMPLETE_PASS` or `COMPLETE_FAIL`; no attempt
`2` exists. Once any prediction byte has been produced, reexecution is
forbidden. After an output hash is committed, one byte-identical upload retry
is permitted only as action `REUPLOAD_COMMITTED_OUTPUT` with reason
`OUTPUT_UPLOAD_FAILURE_AFTER_OUTPUT_HASH_COMMITTED`; this is not reexecution.
There is no third statistical-validation version and no replacement of a
revealed or failed holdout.

## 5. C2: untouched genuine relation invention

### 5.1 Preconditions and isolation

C2 begins only after authoritative C1 PASS. Before any hidden benchmark is
generated, freeze a new C2 preregistration covering:

- synthesizer, source language, quotient semantics, canonical wire, reducer,
  MDL/code table, negative controls, scorer, and all numeric thresholds;
- discovery, validation, and sealed-test cardinalities and allowed data flows;
- keys, custodians, access roles, attempt denominator, retry policy, and
  terminal claim vocabulary;
- old-theory closure and reduction requirements needed to distinguish a new
  relation from a renamed or memorized old relation.

Only after that freeze may an independent custodian generate a new hidden
relation and labels. Neither the development agent nor the relation
synthesizer may access the sealed relation, sealed labels, generator seed, or
custodian key before the one-shot reveal.

### 5.2 Evidence partitions

1. **Discovery:** may be used to synthesize and choose a candidate theory.
2. **Validation:** may select among only preregistered candidate outputs under
   the frozen selection rule.
3. **Sealed test:** is untouched until the final candidate, old-theory
   reduction maps, MDL accounting, and prediction commitment are immutable.

The access ledger must prove that sealed bytes and derived labels were absent
from all synthesis and tuning inputs. The current Generic Odd and hidden-sink
surfaces remain development/null controls and cannot be relabelled as sealed
C2 evidence.

Before the hidden target truth or split roles are available to synthesis, a
target-blind Q1 run must reach a genuinely complete frozen quotient closure
and publish its roots. Q2 may then evaluate bounded target membership against
those immutable Q1 roots. If Q1 is incomplete, over budget, or exposed to
target labels, neither Q2 nor a C2 invention claim is admissible.

### 5.3 C2 pass evidence

- an independently generated hidden benchmark root and one-shot custody log;
- a pre-reveal candidate theory commitment containing any new symbols;
- exact predictions over discovery, validation, and sealed partitions;
- a target-blind Q1 `COMPLETE` certificate followed by a bounded Q2 certificate
  that classifies the target as `OUTSIDE_FROZEN_QUOTIENT_CLOSURE` under the
  same frozen roots;
- proof that the accepted candidate is not merely an old-language expression,
  alias, lookup table, or leaked-label encoding;
- a complete diagnostic ladder covering refit, noise, scope, mixture,
  composition, idealization, robustification, and targeted probes;
- mandatory direct reduction and preservation maps back to the C1 theory;
- frozen MDL/complexity accounting including new-symbol cost;
- a fresh hidden null classified as `IN_LANGUAGE` with zero relation promotion
  and zero false-invention outcome, plus sealed-transfer controls, label
  permutations, leakage probes, and an ablation with the proposed relation
  removed;
- a reveal receipt, frozen scorer output, complete attempt denominator, and
  independently verified terminal report.

`ONTOLOGY_DEFECT` is admissible only after every frozen lower-cost diagnostic
(refit, noise, scope, mixture, composition, idealization, robustification, and
targeted probes) fails to explain the residual, and the remaining defect is
persistent, cross-seed, beyond the frozen uncertainty envelope, structured,
compressible, and predictive on the preregistered holdout. Any missing
predicate leaves the outcome inside the old-language or inconclusive branch.

### 5.4 C2 stop conditions

Pre-freeze benchmark exposure, sealed-partition access, post-reveal model
change, non-authoritative custody, chronology failure, or evidence-integrity
failure terminates as `REJECTED` or `INVALIDATED`. A complete valid run with an
incomplete old-language comparison, invalid scientific MDL result, failed
reduction/preservation threshold, failed negative control, or other frozen
scientific gate terminates as `COMPLETE_FAIL`. C3 remains locked unless the
terminal C2 disposition is authoritative `PASS`.

## 6. C3: multi-generation retention, reduction, rollback, and non-degeneration

### 6.1 Required preregistration

Freeze a versioned multi-generation protocol before creating generation T2:

- append-only theory graph, parent rules, branch identifiers, head selection,
  demotion, revocation, and rollback events;
- exact direct and composed `ReductionMap` semantics;
- ancestor-retention, novelty, complexity/MDL, and non-degeneration metrics;
- independent sealed benchmark allocation per generation;
- rollback trigger, rollback service-level objective, and terminal evidence;
- prohibition on deleting or overwriting a failed generation.

The minimum claim-bearing sequence is `T1(C2) -> T2 -> T3`, with two new
independent sealed targets and one preregistered forced-bad generation or
fault epoch that must trigger rollback. Final cardinalities and thresholds
must be frozen in the C3 preregistration before execution.

### 6.2 C3 pass evidence

- signed append/head records for every theory generation and branch;
- immutable parent, input, implementation, benchmark, and attempt roots;
- per-generation ancestor-retention matrices over all prior accepted tasks;
- direct `Tn -> Tn-1` and composed reduction evidence to T1, T0, and every
  other accepted ancestor required by the frozen theory graph;
- complexity/MDL and non-degeneration vectors under one frozen code table;
- a failed-generation archive and a demonstrated rollback to the last accepted
  head without rewriting history;
- replay from genesis to final head yielding the same accepted/demoted states;
- independent terminal verification of retention, reduction, rollback, and
  non-degeneration gates.

Required negative controls include catastrophic-forgetting patches,
non-transitive reductions, duplicate or renamed theories, constant and
always-abstain outputs, coverage collapse, MDL bloat, evaluator-epoch drift,
stale keys, forks, cycles, concurrent appends, corrupt ancestors, revoked
signers, and a forced-bad generation that superficially improves only the
newest target.

A main accepted-branch run that fails ancestor retention, reduction
composition, non-degeneration, or another frozen scientific gate closes C3 as
`COMPLETE_FAIL` and keeps C4 locked. The preregistered forced-bad generation is
different: it must be rejected, archived, and followed by a successful
rollback; that rejection-plus-rollback is required positive control evidence,
not a C3 failure. Failure to detect the forced-bad generation or failure of its
rollback closes C3 as `COMPLETE_FAIL`, while corrupted custody, chronology, or
ledger integrity is `REJECTED` or `INVALIDATED`. Rollback never decrements or
rewrites the ledger generation: it is a new append-only event binding the bad
head, target good state, trigger evidence, and new monotonic head receipt.

## 7. C4: bounded prospective open-world evolution

“Open-world” is not treated as an unbounded universal claim. C4 must first
freeze a bounded, falsifiable prospective protocol:

- domains, episode count, start/end conditions, time horizon, and denominator;
- raw-input versus typed-input boundary; an independently accepted extractor
  qualification is mandatory for any raw-input claim, and without it the
  strongest permitted result is typed-input only;
- independent source and outcome authorities;
- prediction-before-outcome chronology and authoritative timestamps;
- query, compute, actuation, and safety budgets;
- drift, novelty, calibration, abstention, incident, retention, and rollback
  metrics with exact thresholds;
- allowed interventions and prohibited access channels;
- all retries, missing outcomes, withdrawals, incidents, and abstentions;
- rollback trigger and service-level objective.

Each episode must commit its prediction or action before the outcome authority
releases the corresponding result. Every episode, including failure and
abstention, stays in the denominator. C4 PASS requires the frozen prospective
horizon, all safety constraints, retention/non-degeneration checks, and the
rollback protocol to pass under independent verification. Evidence from local
fixtures or retrospective replay cannot substitute for future outcomes.

Scores from different evaluator epochs are never pooled. Explicit negative
controls must cover ordinary covariate drift without a new relation, noise and
outliers, scope or mixture defects, unit/schema drift, duplicated or replayed
sources, outcome leakage, confounding, contradictory authorities, delayed or
missing outcomes, old-language unknowns, hostile metadata, and unsafe action
requests.

Any provenance, authority, or chronology gap, safety-budget violation,
retention regression, unbounded query or actuation, or rollback-SLA failure
freezes the head and stops new proposals. Episodes without authoritative
outcomes remain `UNSCORABLE` in the denominator. The strongest permitted claim
is a bounded prospective result for the preregistered domains, episodes,
horizon, and budgets; arbitrary scientific discovery, human-level theory
formation, unbounded self-evolution, and production ACTIVE cognition remain
prohibited.

## 8. Local construction versus external evidence

The repository can locally construct and qualify schemas, canonical loaders,
content-ID domains, signature preimages, producer-free verifiers, negative
vectors, crash/concurrency fixtures, and prospective runbooks.

The repository alone cannot establish independent identity, fresh hidden
state, key non-exportability, cross-host one-shot enforcement, external
currentness, rollback-resistant durability, real live-process observations,
actual corpus generation, sealed-data non-access, or future outcome truth.
Those predicates require separately provisioned actors and retained raw
evidence. Until supplied and verified, they remain false.
