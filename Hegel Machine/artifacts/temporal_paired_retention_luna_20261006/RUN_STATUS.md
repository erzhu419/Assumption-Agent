# Published method and availability comparison

Restoring the interrupted paid practice state allows Luna to publish the unchanged
candidate in three additional calls. Its frozen procedure passes 4/4 cases: current
instrument measurements determine prior-predictor reuse or instrument/response
refitting. Both calibrations were previously studied; fitting operators and roles
are supplied. [Publication and frozen execution](../temporal_paired_state_resume_luna_20261006/summary.json).

The live comparison shares original predictive memory, the final verbal lesson,
tools and limits. All eight runs stop before terminal evaluation.

Neither condition falsely confirms. Retention makes seven unchanged-program calls
and preserves the original predictor in both stable cases. The failed retained
case already has a selected candidate but repeatedly edits its manifest after a
missing-path rejection; the failed deleted case never rebuilds the refuted
instrument and also incurs a costly output-decoding failure. Input cache hits total
42.5%. Earlier failed learning costs remain included; their exact token sum is unknown.

## Limitations and next step

Accuracy ties and resource costs trade off; this pilot establishes no overall net
advantage. Direct consumer submission now mechanically registers the explicitly
chosen model before ordinary fresh confirmation. A separate completion of the
original selected candidate passes new 48-row confirmation at MSE 4.23e-20, adding
120 computation units and no LLM calls. This researcher-initiated repair leaves
the original 3/4 result intact. Next test autonomous completion under the shared
repaired interface; further fitting changes are not indicated by this failure.
