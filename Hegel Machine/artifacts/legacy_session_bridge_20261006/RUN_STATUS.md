## Limitations and next step

The removed condition is the same scheduler without transfer knowledge, not a
vanilla Agent. This study establishes knowledge-triggered continuation with greater
research expenditure; it does not establish a cost advantage. The measured source
confirmation and the 12 controls-only successes are distinct outcomes. Next,
preserve the selected callable definition's actual source and binding in its
`transfer_evidence`, which this revision branch currently omits, then use the
existing fresh-comparison and producing-workflow loop. The initial offer and
final callable can use different templates; copying the initial origin would
misattribute the result. The frozen results above remain unchanged.
