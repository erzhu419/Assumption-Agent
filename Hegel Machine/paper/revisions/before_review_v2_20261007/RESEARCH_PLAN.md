## Paper thesis

A hypothesis is a scoped abstraction, and its supporting assumptions can become
research questions in their own right. The framework pairs possible explanations
with observations that make their differences visible. Recursive research can
revise either side, including the representation, applicability conditions and
procedure used to investigate the parent question. A broader explanation should
preserve earlier supported behavior within its valid scope.

The first paper establishes bounded mechanisms within that account: child evidence
changes pending experiments, measurement access restores missing distinctions,
research procedures improve frozen transfer, and retaining failed computations
supports repair and return. This does not require completing every capability of
the original vision. General space invention, cross-domain structural discovery
and a unique advantage over an equally capable ordinary archive remain open.

## Terms and the argument

A hypothesis gives executable predictions within an explicit scope. A research
program chooses measurements, experiments or subproblems from the current evidence.
A meta-prior is knowledge that guides these choices across tasks and can itself
be revised. Recursive research makes an unresolved supporting obligation into a
research question and can apply that process again to its dependencies. The
current nested-relation path fits through prescribed operators, records completed
steps in a dependency graph, and performs bounded experimental refinement.
The graph alone is not evidence of autonomous recursive inquiry.

Learning a callable abstraction can improve search without extending the expressive
power of the underlying programming language. The manuscript should distinguish
this effective research-language growth from strict representational impossibility.
Conservative revision means retaining supported behavior within its valid scope;
it does not require retaining disproved conclusions or proving a logical theorem.

The method section should connect residuals to research decisions, acquired evidence,
local revision and subsequent reuse. The results should then test whether that
connection changes quality and resource use, rather than count implemented modules.

## What the current evidence supports

The [composition study](../docs/hegel_machine_composed_local_cycle_20261004.md)
shows independently learned components being combined and locally revised in one
controlled object family. Its frozen candidate completed 11 of 12 strict cycles;
both retention and source-deletion arms solved 72 of 72 terminal tasks. Retention
used fewer observations, measurements and charged computation in all 12 pairs.
This supports a causal benefit of retaining A and its downstream knowledge under
that protocol. It does not separate the contributions of every source.

The subsequent [four-arm comparison](../artifacts/paper_composed_comparison_20261005/summary.json)
completed 30 new apparatus instances, with all four algorithms evaluated on 180
tasks. Full solved 180/180; the same researcher initialized independently on each
task solved 179/180. Full used 59.02% fewer observations, 74.82% fewer measurement
units and 76.25% less charged computation. Its cumulative training CPU time was
24.17% lower, but the paired interval includes zero: one revisit incurred a large
runtime cost. Passive research solved 87/180, and the current dense fixed-basis
fit solved 0/180. All failures are retained. The main supported gain is research
resource use relative to task-local learning, with nearly equal accuracy.

The [retention intervention](../artifacts/paper_scoped_retention_20261005/summary.json)
completed another 30 apparatus instances, with all challenges activating. Retaining
the observed-failing response, with or without its conditional-feedback record,
solved 180/180 tasks; deleting it solved 159/180. Both retained arms used identical
charged resources, saving 20.00% observations, 39.04% measurements and 26.59%
computation against deletion over complete sequences. All arms solved the primary
original-return task in every instance; retention reduced return computation by
77.66%. The additional conditional record had no independent measured benefit.
Deletion also produced four internally confirmed first-revision candidates that
failed terminal evaluation, while other failures remained unresolved with resources
left. These findings make evidence-directed revision the next empirical question.

The single-source comparison also contains a material trade-off: `fixed_library`
and full each solved 48/48 tasks, but charged computation was 20,736 versus 318,674,
while measurements were 9,216 versus 2,448. Keep this result in the manuscript and
report resource dimensions separately. It does not establish overall dominance.

## Later research

Unseen structural families, real domain transfer, invention of new experimental
tools, reliable uncertainty calibration and per-step procedure attribution remain
extensions. Longer sequences must vary recurrence and change frequency to test
whether learning repays its acquisition and maintenance costs. The current
charged-resource savings include acquisition but coexist with a CPU penalty;
they do not imply stable long-horizon benefit. The earlier 12-seed prototype
is preserved in its [historical report](../docs/hegel_machine_joint_growth_20261006.md)
and superseded for paper claims by the stronger 30-seed protocol.

The common-model comparison is now part of the current experimental program.
Open-world discovery and universal cognitive claims remain beyond the current evidence.
Further optimizing the existing 11/12 direct-call result is secondary unless it
changes the first paper's central conclusion.
