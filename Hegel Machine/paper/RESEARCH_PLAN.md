## Paper thesis

A hypothesis is a scoped abstraction, and its supporting assumptions can become
research questions in their own right. The framework pairs possible explanations
with observations that make their differences visible. Recursive research can
revise either side, including the representation, applicability conditions and
procedure used to investigate the parent question. A broader explanation should
preserve earlier supported behavior within its valid scope.

The first paper establishes bounded mechanisms within that account: child evidence
changes pending experiments, measurement access restores missing distinctions,
research procedures improve frozen transfer, and retaining failed computations
supports repair and return. This does not require completing every capability of
the original vision. General space invention, cross-domain structural discovery
and a unique advantage over an equally capable ordinary archive remain open.

## Terms and the argument

A hypothesis gives executable predictions within an explicit scope. A research
program chooses measurements, experiments or subproblems from the current evidence.
A meta-prior is knowledge that guides these choices across tasks and can itself
be revised. Recursive research makes an unresolved supporting obligation into a
research question and can apply that process again to its dependencies. The
current nested-relation path fits through prescribed operators, records completed
steps in a dependency graph, and performs bounded experimental refinement.
The graph alone is not evidence of autonomous recursive inquiry.

Learning a callable abstraction can improve search without extending the expressive
power of the underlying programming language. The manuscript should distinguish
this effective research-language growth from strict representational impossibility.
Conservative revision means retaining supported behavior within its valid scope;
it does not require retaining disproved conclusions or proving a logical theorem.

The method section should connect residuals to research decisions, acquired evidence,
local revision and subsequent reuse. The results should then test whether that
connection changes quality and resource use, rather than count implemented modules.
